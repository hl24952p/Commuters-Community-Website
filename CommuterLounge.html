Commuter Lounge!

All commuter students have access to the new commuter lounge located on the second floor of Mortola Library!
This is a space that commuters can go to in between lasses to relax, unwind, do work, catch up with friends,
and you may even see commuter advisory board hosting an event in there every once in a while. We have various resources
in the lounge and are always looking for feedback on how to improve it!
Please email us with any suggestions you may have!

Events

CAB Events Hosted Throughout the Year

General Body Meeting

Interested in becoming a member of CAB? Join us for our bi-weekly general body meetings in Kessel MPR. You will be able to be a part of the event planning process, get member exclusive perks, and immerse yourself into the commuter community! Each week we will have different activities, and we can't wait to see you there!

Tie-Dye and Ice Cream Cones

Join CAB to tie-dye a custom commuter t-shirt (designed by you) & enjoy some free ice cream from the mister softee ice cream truck!

Commuter Spa Day

Come join Cosmetology club & CAB for a relaxing spa day! The event will be held at the Commuter Lounge during common hour. Indulge in facials, lip care, and nail care to relax and rejuvenate in between class. Self-Care Giveaways & PaceFit Acai Bowls will be Provided!

Commuter Appreciation Week - Roller Rink

Come Celebrate Commuter Appreciation Day with our Roller Rink, DJ, Photo booth, Catering from Moe's Southwest Grill, and Exclusive Raffles in Gottesman from 12pm to 3pm!!!

Halloween Game Show & Happy Hour

Join CAB and celebrate spooky spirit week with us with a Halloween gameshow! You can win real CASH prizes! We will also have food and mocktails!

Puppy Yoga

Want to destress and play with some puppies? You have come to the right place! Commuter Advisory Board will be bringing puppy yoga to campus, just for commuters! Enjoy a puppy yoga class, and puppy playtime on Tuesday, April 9th in celebration of Commuter Appreciation Week!

Friendsgiving with Programming Board

Join us for a special Friendsgiving celebration hosted by Programming Board and Commuter Advisory Board! Enjoy great food, fun activities, and an opportunity to connect with fellow students as we come together in the spirit of friendship and community. All students are welcome to share in the festivities!

Gingerbread Making Competition

Join Commuter Street Team for a Gingerbread House decorating competition! 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place winners will receive a prize! There will also be gingerbread men cookies to decorate! Hot Cocoa will be served. Can't wait to see you there!

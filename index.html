Pace University's Commuter Advisory Board

"Commuter Advisory Board (CAB) is a student run organization on the Pleasantville campus.
We work to increase commuter student engagement on campus by planning fun, interactive,
and educational commuter-specific events. CAB strives to advocate for the commuter student population,
their needs, and works to promote on-campus opportunities for them. CABs overall goal is to help create the
best college experience for our commuter students and support them along their educational journey here at Pace."
